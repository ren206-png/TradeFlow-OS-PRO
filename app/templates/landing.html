Questions

Good things to ask.

How does TradeFlow work with my existing number?
You keep your number. We give you a dedicated TradeFlow number, and you forward your calls to it with a short dialer code (we show you the exact one for your carrier). Customers keep calling the number they already know.
Can my phone ring first?
Yes, that's the default. Your phone rings as usual, and if you don't pick up, the AI answers. If you'd rather the AI take every call, you can choose that instead. Some carriers send unanswered calls to their own voicemail first. If yours does, ask them to switch voicemail off for that line, or choose the "every call" option.
Will callers know it's an AI?
Yes. It introduces itself as an AI assistant and says the call may be recorded. In practice, callers mostly care that someone picked up, asked sensible questions and booked the job.
What about emergencies?
The AI recognises urgent situations like flooding, no heat in winter or a gas smell. It books the earliest slot, and if you've turned on emergency transfers it rings your mobile and puts the caller straight through. It will also put anyone through who asks for a person.
Which languages does it speak?
English by default. If a caller speaks French or Spanish, the AI switches to that language for the whole conversation and still sends you your summary in English.
What trades is it for?
Plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, roofers, garage door and locksmith companies, towing and general contractors. If you take calls and book jobs, it fits.
What happens after the free trial?
You pick a plan: Starter at $5.99 a month or Pro at $97 a month, both billed in US dollars with no contract. We'll email you before the trial ends. If you don't subscribe, your AI line simply pauses; nothing is charged.
